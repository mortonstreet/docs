title: "Campaigns"
description: "From ranked candidates to Smartlead/HeyReach outreach — with a human launch gate"
---

Campaigns (`/campaigns`) turns past searches into outreach. The LLM only ever drafts
the plan; every API call against Smartlead or HeyReach is a fixed, ordered step shown
in a live ledger.

## Building a campaign

1. **Pick lead sources** — one or more completed searches; leads are deduped across
   them.
2. **Describe the campaign** — platform, volume, schedule, tone ("HeyReach, 20
   leads/day, weekdays 9–5 Detroit time, friendly 3-step sequence…"). The model
   selector (Haiku/Sonnet/Opus) sets copywriting strength.
3. **Review the plan** — platform, schedule, eligible-lead counts, preflight issues
   (missing API keys, no connected senders, no eligible contacts), and the full copy.

## You control the copy — three ways

- **Verbatim**: paste your own copy in the prompt ("use this exact message: …") — it's
  used exactly, never rewritten; the model only writes steps you left unwritten.
- **Edit before publish**: the connection note (with 280-character counter) and every
  step message in the preview are editable. What you see is what publishes.
- **Skip copy here entirely**: **Push list only** creates a HeyReach list with the
  selected leads and no campaign — build the sequence in HeyReach's own builder.

## The launch gate

Publishing stages everything — list, leads, sequence, schedule — and then **stops**.
The campaign shows "ready to launch" with nothing sent; on HeyReach it exists as a
reviewable draft. The explicit **Launch campaign** click is the approval (stamped with
who and when). Nothing ever sends without a human go.

## After launch

Live campaigns show leads pushed and the external campaign ID. Past campaigns keep
their full step ledger, including the exact provider error on any failed step.
