Open Account Settings from your name at the bottom of the sidebar (`/settings`).

## Access is invite-only

Revcenter has no public signup. Accounts are created exclusively through workspace
invitations — an admin invites an email from the Team tab, the recipient follows the
emailed link and verifies with a one-time code. A sign-in attempt from an email with
no pending invitation is rejected. Sign-in itself is passwordless: existing users
request a magic link from the login page.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **General** | Workspace name and export policy: allow/disallow single and bulk exports for members, and optionally restrict exports to leads with contact info. |
| **Profile** | Your name and email. |
| **Team** | Members and roles, pending invitations, invite by email. Admin/member roles control who can run searches, campaigns, and exports. |
| **Do-Not-Contact** | Exclusion rules — companies and countries that must never appear in results or exports. |

## Admin pages (sidebar → Admin)

- **Credentials** — add, rotate, and deactivate API keys (Serper, HarvestAPI,
  Anthropic, Prospeo, Smartlead, HeyReach). Keys are encrypted at rest; database
  keys take effect immediately and win over environment configuration. See
  [API Keys](/guides/api-keys).
- **Gates** — per-stage approval behavior for scans (auto-approve vs. require
  approval).
- **Tenants** (Revcenter team only) — workspace provisioning and the dev workspace
  switcher.

## Frozen accounts

An administrator can freeze an account: the user can still sign in, but the entire
app is view-disabled with a banner until unfrozen.
